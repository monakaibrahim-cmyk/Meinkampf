# Bedrock QoL Suite — v1.0.4 Release Notes

**Release Version:** `v1.0.4`  
**Compatibility:** Minecraft Bedrock Edition 1.21.0+ (`@minecraft/server` 2.10.0, `@minecraft/server-ui` 2.2.0)

---

## Highlights & New Features

### 1. Campfire Defense & Healing Area
* **Passive & Neutral Mob Healing:** Lit campfires provide gentle health regeneration to players, pets, and neutral/passive mobs in their radius.
* **Hostile Deterrent & Debuffs:** Hostile mobs entering the perimeter take burning damage and receive status debuffs (Slowness, Weakness, Mining Fatigue, and Blindness).
* **Smart Exemption:** Named hostile mobs (e.g. mob displays, trapped nametagged mobs) are exempted to prevent accidental mob farm disruptions.
* **Multi-Campfire Perimeters:** Seamless perimeter calculations across clusters of campfires.

### 2. AFK Protection System
* **Idle Safety:** Players standing idle for a configured duration gain temporary invincibility and mob untargetability to guard against sudden mob spawns while away.
* **Realm / Server Kick Config:** Optional automatic disconnection on dedicated servers or realms, with automatic exemption for local singleplayer worlds.
* **AI & Simulated Player Support:** Fully compatible with simulated players and bots.

### 3. Quick Sorter & Inventory Refinements
* **New World Granting:** Players starting a brand new world are reliably granted their starter Quick Sorter scepter on first spawn.
* **Custom 3D Item Model:** Enhanced Quick Sorter visual appearance with a custom 3D scepter model and polished icon.
* **Storage-Only Sorting:** Auto-sorting and the Quick Sorter exclusively sort pure storage blocks (Chests, Trapped Chests, Barrels, Shulker Boxes). Active processing containers such as Furnaces, Blast Furnaces, Smokers, Hoppers, Droppers, and Dispensers are safely exempted.

### 4. Offhand Compatibility Safeguards
* **Retained Offhand Survival Items:** Offhand utility is actively supported for essential survival tools:
  * **Auto Water Bucket:** MLG water bucket clutches trigger from the offhand, swap to an empty bucket, and replenish upon picking the water back up.
  * **Auto Totem of Undying:** Protects against fatal blows and void falls from the offhand, with stack-safe deduction preserving remainder totems.
  * **Passive Potions:** Holding beneficial potions in the offhand grants passive status buffs and regeneration.
  * **Auto Shield:** Detects offhand shields for auto-blocking and supports optional auto-equipping into empty offhands.
* **Reliable Offhand Torches & Dynamic Lighting:**
  * **Fix for Vanilla Stack-to-1 Bug:** Solved the Bedrock client-side block placement prediction bug that previously truncated torch stacks to 1 by using Sneak + Hit (`entityHitBlock`) for placing light blocks from offhand.
  * **Air Right-Click Quick-Equip:** Right-clicking in the air automatically transfers torches, lanterns, or candles from the main hand into the offhand slot with reliable API and command fallbacks.
  * **Dual-Hand Dynamic Lighting:** Dynamic lighting seamlessly computes light levels from both main hand and offhand items simultaneously.
  * **Automatic Stack Merging:** Background inventory sync automatically combines fragmented torch and light item stacks.

### 5. Tree Capitator & Natural World Refinements
* **Fallen Log Sapling Fix:** Felling naturally fallen trees and downed logs lying on the ground now exclusively yields log drops without planting saplings, since fallen trees lack leaves and produce no sapling drops.
* **Creeper Daylight Mechanics:** Morning daylight checks and standard daylight burning mechanics for creepers to clean up leftover nighttime spawns.

### 6. Smart Elytra <--> Armor Switcher (PC & Mobile Overhaul)
* **Cross-Platform Responsive Flight:** Re-engineered mid-air flight triggers to eliminate PC spacebar lag and mobile touch-button dropped inputs.
* **Airborne Jump Detection:** Tapping Jump in mid-air immediately equips the player's Elytra so wings deploy seamlessly.
* **Mid-Air Firework Detection:** Holding a Firework Rocket while airborne instantly equips the Elytra, ensuring takeoff propulsion works on the very first rocket click/tap without wasting fireworks.
* **Bunny-Hop & Auto-Jump Immune:** Calibrated fall distance (1.8+ blocks) prevents normal 1-block ground jumps and mobile Auto-Jumps from accidentally equipping Elytra.
* **Safe Touchdown Restoral:** Automatically restores chestplate when grounded for 4+ ticks, eliminating the trapped-in-elytra bug.
* **Sneak + Right-Click / Tap in Air:** Effortless manual hotkey toggle across both PC (Shift + Right-Click air) and Mobile (Crouch + Tap air).

### 7. Silk Touch Spawners & VeinMiner Compatibility
* **Silk Touch Mob Spawner Harvesting:** Mine and collect `minecraft:mob_spawner` blocks using a pickaxe with Silk Touch (Iron tier or higher).
* **Entity Type Retention & Spawn Egg Drops:** Detects nearby mob types (Zombie, Skeleton, Spider, Cave Spider, Blaze, Silverfish, Magma Cube) upon harvest. In addition to a lore-tagged Mob Spawner block, drops the matching spawn egg so players can immediately restore their mob spawner type upon placement in survival.
* **VeinMiner Integration:** Full compatibility with VeinMiner. Mining clusters of spawners with Silk Touch safely cascades and harvests each spawner with appropriate lore and spawn eggs, while non-Silk Touch mining cleanly awards 15-43 XP per spawner without duplicate drops.
* **Lit Redstone Ore VeinMiner Fix:** Fixed an issue where Silk-touching lit redstone ores during a VeinMiner cascade yielded missing-texture `lit_redstone_ore` items; now cleanly drops standard `minecraft:redstone_ore` and `minecraft:deepslate_redstone_ore`.
* **Auto Elytra Switcher Config:** Disabled by default (`enabled: false`) pending further manual in-game testing.

### 8. Auto Eat System (Smart Nutrition & Vanilla Balancing)
* **Progressive Vanilla Eating Cycle:** Replaced instant food consumption with an authentic 32-tick (1.6-second) chewing sequence matching vanilla Minecraft pacing.
* **Survival Movement Balancing:** Applies realistic movement slowdown while eating to prevent unbalancing combat and sprint-glitching.
* **Vanilla Eating Animation & Feedback:** Triggers humanoid hand-to-mouth arm animation, periodic chewing crunch sounds, mouth particles, and a live Action Bar progress bar (`[■■■■□□□□]`).
* **Intelligent Hunger & Saturation Management:** Automatically initiates eating from offhand, hotbar, or backpack when hunger drops below threshold (default 14) or when injured (maintains hunger >= 18 for continuous natural health regeneration).
* **Emergency Golden Apple Rescue:** Immediately consumes Golden Apples or Enchanted Golden Apples when taking severe damage or reaching critical health (<= 4 hearts), even if the hunger bar is completely full.
* **Smart Food Scoring & Safety:** Prioritizes high-saturation foods (Golden Carrots, Cooked Meats) and protects players by excluding harmful items (Rotten Flesh, Spider Eyes, Pufferfish, Poisonous Potatoes) and Chorus Fruit by default.
* **Container Restoral:** Returns empty wooden bowls (from stews and soups) and glass bottles (from honey bottles) back to the player's inventory.
