# Bedrock QoL Suite — v1.0.4 Release Notes

**Release Version:** `v1.0.4`  
**Compatibility:** Minecraft Bedrock Edition 1.21.0+ (`@minecraft/server` 2.10.0, `@minecraft/server-ui` 2.2.0)

---

## Highlights & New Features

### 1. Campfire Defense & Healing Area
* **Passive & Neutral Mob Healing:** Lit campfires provide gentle health regeneration to players, pets, and neutral/passive mobs in their radius.
* **Hostile Deterrent & Debuffs:** Hostile mobs entering the perimeter take burning damage and receive status debuffs (Slowness, Weakness, Mining Fatigue, and Blindness).
* **Smart Exemption:** Named hostile mobs (e.g. mob displays, trapped nametagged mobs) are exempted to prevent accidental mob farm disruptions.
* **Multi-Campfire Perimeters:** Seamless perimeter calculations across clusters of campfires.

### 2. AFK Protection System
* **Idle Safety:** Players standing idle for a configured duration gain temporary invincibility and mob untargetability to guard against sudden mob spawns while away.
* **Realm / Server Kick Config:** Optional automatic disconnection on dedicated servers or realms, with automatic exemption for local singleplayer worlds.
* **AI & Simulated Player Support:** Fully compatible with simulated players and bots.

### 3. Quick Sorter & Inventory Refinements
* **New World Granting:** Players starting a brand new world are reliably granted their starter Quick Sorter scepter on first spawn.
* **Custom 3D Item Model:** Enhanced Quick Sorter visual appearance with a custom 3D scepter model and polished icon.
* **Storage-Only Sorting:** Auto-sorting and the Quick Sorter exclusively sort pure storage blocks (Chests, Trapped Chests, Barrels, Shulker Boxes). Active processing containers such as Furnaces, Blast Furnaces, Smokers, Hoppers, Droppers, and Dispensers are safely exempted.

### 4. Offhand Compatibility Safeguards
* **Retained Offhand Survival Items:** Offhand utility is actively supported for essential survival tools:
  * **Auto Water Bucket:** MLG water bucket clutches trigger from the offhand, swap to an empty bucket, and replenish upon picking the water back up.
  * **Auto Totem of Undying:** Protects against fatal blows and void falls from the offhand, with stack-safe deduction preserving remainder totems.
  * **Passive Potions:** Holding beneficial potions in the offhand grants passive status buffs and regeneration.
  * **Auto Shield:** Detects offhand shields for auto-blocking and supports optional auto-equipping into empty offhands.
* **Reliable Offhand Torches & Dynamic Lighting:**
  * **Fix for Vanilla Stack-to-1 Bug:** Solved the Bedrock client-side block placement prediction bug that previously truncated torch stacks to 1 by using Sneak + Hit (`entityHitBlock`) for placing light blocks from offhand.
  * **Air Right-Click Quick-Equip:** Right-clicking in the air automatically transfers torches, lanterns, or candles from the main hand into the offhand slot with reliable API and command fallbacks.
  * **Dual-Hand Dynamic Lighting:** Dynamic lighting seamlessly computes light levels from both main hand and offhand items simultaneously.
  * **Automatic Stack Merging:** Background inventory sync automatically combines fragmented torch and light item stacks.

### 5. Creeper Daylight Mechanics
* Morning daylight checks and standard daylight burning mechanics for creepers to clean up leftover nighttime spawns.

### 6. Automation & Publishing
* Automated CurseForge release pipeline for verified Bedrock builds.
* Addon-focused release notes generation for Discord webhooks and GitHub Releases.
