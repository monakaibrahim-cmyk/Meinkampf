# Bedrock QoL Suite — v1.0.1 Patch Notes

Maintenance patch introducing a standalone custom Quick Sorter item, inventory tier/quality sorting, death retention and anti-duplication, lingering campfire defense, offhand balance, and indicator stability improvements.

---

## Changes & Fixes

### 1. Auto Totem
* **Removed Auto Offhand Populating**: The automatic offhand refill mechanic (`refillOffhandTotem`) has been completely removed.
* Players can now keep their offhand slot empty or freely hold shields, torches, lanterns, maps, food, or fireworks without totems being automatically forced into the slot.
* **Preserved Protection**: Totem of Undying protection continues to save players from fatal damage and void falls directly from anywhere in the hotbar, main hand, offhand, or backpack.
* **Stacking**: Totem stacking up to 64 items per slot remains active.

### 2. Custom Quick Sorter Item & Anti-Duplication
* **Dedicated Custom Item & Icon**: Replaced the placeholder compass with a brand new custom item (`qol:quick_sorter`) featuring its own 32x32 pixel art icon, item textures, and glint effect.
* **Retained on Death**: The Quick Sorter item now has `keepOnDeath: true` enabled. When a player dies, the tool stays securely in their inventory rather than dropping on the ground or disappearing.
* **Inventory Lock**: Quick Sorter is assigned `lockMode: inventory`, preventing accidental item dropping or crafting consumption.
* **Anti-Duplication**: Prevents duplicate sorter tools from being re-given upon respawning. Any dropped sorter item entities detected on the ground are automatically removed. Existing legacy compass sorters are seamlessly upgraded in-place.

### 3. Inventory & Container Sorting
* **New "Quality / Tier" Sorting Mode**: Added a new `"quality"` sorting mode to `defaultSortType`, the Container Sorter GUI, and the Backpack Sorter GUI.
  * Organizes items by material tier and gear value: Netherite > Diamond > Iron/Chainmail > Gold > Copper > Stone > Wood/Leather.
  * Places ultimate relics and treasures (Elytra, Nether Star, Totem of Undying, Mace, Heavy Core, Enchanted Golden Apples, Shulkers) at the top.
  * Analyzes item enchantments (`minecraft:enchantable`) to prioritize high-level enchanted equipment.
* **Removed Crouch Right-Click Interception on Chests**: Sneak-interacting on chests, barrels, hoppers, and shulker boxes is no longer intercepted.
* Crouching players can freely place hoppers into chests, place blocks on containers, and attach signs, frames, or torches without opening the sorter GUI.
* **Direct Sorter Item Interaction**: Right-clicking a chest or container while holding the **⚡ Quick Sorter** tool opens the Container Sorter menu directly.
* Right-clicking in the air or on non-container blocks opens the Backpack Sorter menu.
* **Configuration Cleanup**: Removed deprecated `sneakInteractContainer` configuration property.

### 4. Dynamic Lighting Cleanup
* **Removed Sneak-Swing Mechanic**: Removed `sneakSwingToEquip` and `showEquipNotification` from `DynamicLightingConfig` and runtime listeners.
* Equipping torches and lanterns into the offhand is handled cleanly through the Quick Sorter Backpack GUI button (`Torch / Lantern Offhand`).

### 5. Campfire Regeneration & Defense
* **Out-of-Range Lingering Burn**: Lit campfires keep hostile mobs within `burnHostilesRadius` continuously ignited (1s) and pushed back. The extended `burnHostileDurationSeconds` burn duration is now applied exclusively once the hostile mob is pushed or moves **out of range** from `burnHostilesRadius`.
* **3x Campfire Damage to Hostiles**: Lit campfires and soul campfires now deal 3x damage against all hostile mobs (`hostileDamageMultiplier: 3.0`), dealing 3 HP (1.5 hearts) per hit on campfires and 6 HP (3 hearts) on soul campfires.
* Intercepts both direct campfire block contact and fire burn damage when mobs stand on lit campfires.

### 6. Neutral Mob Nameplates
* **Always-Visible Nameplates**: Fixed an issue where neutral mobs (Iron Golems, Wolves, Bees, Piglins, Zombified Piglins, Polar Bears, Goats, Llamas, Dolphins, Pandas, Snow Golems) only displayed their health card nameplate when directly targeted with the player's crosshair.
* **Behavior Pack Data Module**: Added the required `data` module to `behavior_pack/manifest.json` so Minecraft Bedrock actively loads entity definitions containing `"always_show": true`.
* **Registry & API Updates**: Added `minecraft:zombie_pigman` to the neutral mob registry and set `nameplateRenderDistance: 64` directly to render nameplates cleanly up to 64 blocks away without crosshair aiming.

### 7. Crop Growth Indicator
* **Static Billboard Display**: Fixed an issue where the floating exclamation mark (`!`) growth indicator would periodically disappear, blink, and reappear above crops.
* **Extended Particle Lifetime**: Increased green, yellow, and red indicator particle lifetime from `0.85s` to `2.2s`, with a steady `20-tick` (`1.0s`) server refresh loop, ensuring a continuous `1.2s` overlap buffer without server tick jitter.
* **Complete Farm Grid Scanning**: Farmland area discovery scan updated to evaluate all continuous block coordinates (`x++`, `z++`), ensuring no crop rows or columns are skipped when approaching farms.
