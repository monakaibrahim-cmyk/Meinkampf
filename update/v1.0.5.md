# Bedrock QoL Suite — v1.0.5 Release Notes

**Release Version:** `v1.0.5`  
**Compatibility:** Minecraft Bedrock Edition 1.21.0+ (`@minecraft/server` 2.10.0, `@minecraft/server-ui` 2.2.0)

---

## Bug Fixes & Stability Updates

### 1. Seaweed (Kelp) Infinite Vertical Ocean & Sky Water Generation Fix
* **Strict Water-Only Growth:** Fixed an issue where kelp (seaweed) continued growing past the ocean surface into open air blocks. Kelp growth now strictly requires `nextBlock.typeId === "minecraft:water"`, preventing kelp from ever breaching the surface or growing into the sky.
* **No Floating Water Sources:** Corrected kelp harvesting (both automated chest harvesting and manual right-click harvesting) so that upper stalk blocks are only restored to water if they are actually submerged within an existing water column. Any segments exposed to open air are safely replaced with air, preventing runaway ocean layer generation up to world height limit.
* **Submerged Validation on Discovery:** Both world scanning and block placement listeners now verify that kelp stalks are rooted on solid ground submerged underwater before registering for growth acceleration.

### 2. Container Sorting (Chests, Barrels & Shulker Boxes) Full Overhaul
* **Live Container Re-Acquisition:** Fixed an issue where container sorting failed due to stale native `Container` handles expiring between form display and button selection. The sorter now dynamically re-fetches live, valid container references at the exact moment of sorting.
* **Reliable Auto-Sort on Container Close:** Fixed `blockContainerClosed` by deferring slot reordering to the next tick via `system.run()`. This prevents native engine `ContainerRulesError` lockouts that previously blocked containers from auto-sorting upon being closed.
* **Natural Container Mining & Destruction:** Disallowed punching or attacking containers from opening the Sorter form. Players can now mine, break, and relocate chests, barrels, and shulker boxes normally with any tool or bare hands without modal UI interruptions. Container sorting is accessed cleanly by right-clicking with the Quick Sorter or via auto-sort on close.
* **Targeted Chest Button in Backpack GUI:** When viewing the Backpack Sorter menu while looking at a chest within 5 blocks, a dedicated `"Sort Targeted Chest"` button now appears, allowing seamless chest sorting directly from the scepter interface.
* **Perpendicular Double Chest Pairing:** Refined `getConnectedChests` to only look for paired chests along the perpendicular axis to their facing direction (e.g. East/West for North-facing chests). This prevents chests in adjacent rows or behind each other from being incorrectly merged into double chests.
* **Safe Slot Write-Back:** Added individual slot try-catch error protection during item array write-back in `performSort` to guarantee graceful handling of any slot restrictions.
### 3. Player-Planted Only Fast Crop Growth & 3.25x Speed Multiplier
* **Player-Planted Restriction:** Crop growth acceleration now strictly applies *only* to crops planted, tilled, or harvested by players. Naturally generated wild vegetation (wild taiga/marsh sweet berry bushes, wild kelp, wild sugar cane, wild cacti, bamboo jungles, jungle cocoa pods, cave vines in lush caves, and village farmlands) is completely excluded from fast growth.
* **Wild Scanning Removed:** Removed wild stalk, vine, cocoa, and berry scanning from area discovery loops, eliminating terrain scan lag and memory bloat across newly loaded chunks.
* **3.25x Growth Multiplier:** Increased crop growth speed multiplier to `3.25x` (`growthRateMultiplier: 3.25`), reducing full maturation time to ~6.15 minutes (7,385 ticks) while maintaining consistent server performance.
* **Fixed Rapid Growth Marsh Crashes:** Removed legacy berry-specific loops (`berriesFastGrowth`, `berriesFullGrowthTicks`, `growthCheckIntervalTicks`) that caused severe lag and crashes in dense marsh biomes.
* **Stabilized 5-Second Interval:** Growth checks run every 100 ticks (5.0 seconds) across loaded dimensions.
* **Lily Pad Exemption:** Explicitly exempted lily pads (`minecraft:waterlily` / `minecraft:lily_pad`) from crop discovery, farmland registration, interaction harvesting, and fast growth processing.

### 4. Quick Sorter Icon
* **New Hotbar & Inventory Icon:** Replaced the old thin wand sprite with a bolder 16x16 pixel-art scepter (gold staff, cyan crystal head, dark outline). It is upscaled to 32x32 so it stays readable in the hotbar.
* **Icon Not Showing Fix:** Moved the item icon to the 1.21 `minecraft:icon` format (`textures.default`) and removed the old resource pack item file that could stop the icon from loading.

### 5. Modern Continuous Health Bar Nameplate Card
* **Seamless Continuous Health Bar:** Replaced legacy segmented pill chunks with a single solid, continuous health bar featuring a black border and smooth rounded caps at the ends, matching the clean modern template card design.
* **Pixel-Exact 3-Tier Shading:** Each fill state rendered with exact 11-row pixel-art shading: top highlight tint, vibrant main body, bottom shadow, and dark gray empty track.
* **Dynamic HP Color Matching:** Current HP readout on Line 1 dynamically matches the health bar color: Green (`§a`) for high health (> 50%), Yellow (`§e`) for mid health (25%–50%), and Red (`§c`) for low health (≤ 25%).
* **Balanced Full Width:** Line 1 text (mob name and HP fraction) automatically pads flush with the 86-pixel health bar across all mobs.
