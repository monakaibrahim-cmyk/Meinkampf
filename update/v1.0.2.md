# Bedrock QoL Suite — v1.0.2 Patch Notes

Hotfix update resolving chest punch destruction, offhand torch/lantern equipment, continuous campfire defense damage against hostile mobs, and always-visible nameplates for zombie villagers, neutral, and passive mobs.

---

## Changes & Fixes

### 1. Chest Punch Destruction & Container Sorting Trigger
* **Normal Chest Breaking**: Removed punch/attack interception (`entityHitBlock`) on chests and containers. Punching a chest with bare hands, an axe, a pickaxe, or any tool now breaks and destroys the chest as expected instead of bringing up the sorting menu.
* **Dedicated Sorter Item Interaction**: Container sorting options are now accessed exclusively by equipping the **⚡ Quick Sorter** item (in main hand or offhand) and right-clicking on the chest or container.
* **Config Update**: Default `hitContainerToOpenMenu` set to `false`.

### 2. Offhand Torches & Lanterns
* **Offhand Light Equipping**: Torches, soul torches, redstone torches, underwater torches, colored torches, lanterns, soul lanterns, sea lanterns, and jack-o'-lanterns are freely equipable in the offhand (shield / left hand) slot.
* **Backpack Sorter GUI Button**: Dedicated `Torch / Lantern Offhand` button in the Quick Sorter menu allows players to instantly equip or swap light sources into the offhand slot from anywhere in their inventory.
* **Right-Click Air Use**: Right-clicking into empty air while holding a torch or lantern equips/swaps it into the offhand slot.
* **Clean Interaction**: Removed sneak-swinging and command macros to avoid interaction conflicts during combat, mining, and building. Action bar notification and authentic equipment audio confirm offhand swaps.

### 3. Continuous Campfire Defense Damage to Hostiles
* **Indefinite In-Radius Damage**: Fixed hostile mobs inside lit campfire `burnHostilesRadius` (10 blocks) to take damage continuously/indefinitely each second (20 ticks) for the entire time they remain inside the radius, rather than only catching a brief fire timer or taking damage only when directly stepping on top of the campfire block.
* **Campfire Damage Multiplier**: Deals 3 HP per hit on lit campfires and 6 HP per hit on soul campfires with 3x damage multiplier.
* **Knockback & Particles**: Maintains slight radial knockback and flame particle sizzles each second while inside the defensive perimeter.

### 4. Always-Visible Nameplates for Zombie Villagers, Neutral & Passive Mobs
* **Zombie Villager Support**: Added `minecraft:zombie_villager_v2` and `minecraft:evocation_illager` to the hostile mob registry and nameplate formatters.
* **Passive Mobs Behavior Pack Definitions**: Generated complete `always_show: true` entity definitions for all 30+ passive mobs (cows, sheep, pigs, chickens, horses, donkeys, mules, villagers, villager_v2, wandering traders, cats, ocelots, parrots, rabbits, foxes, bats, striders, axolotls, glow squids, squids, turtles, allays, frogs, tadpoles, camels, sniffers, armadillos, mooshrooms, skeleton horses, zombie horses).
* **Neutral & Zombified Piglin Definitions**: Configured `zombified_piglin.json` and ensured all neutral mobs feature `"always_show": true`.
* **Crosshair-Free Rendering**: Nameplates for all hostile, neutral, and passive mobs now render continuously up to 64 blocks away without requiring the player to target the entity with their crosshair or be 1–2 blocks near it.

### 5. Signless Auto-Farm & Automatic Seed Composting
* **Signless Farmland Chests**: Crops no longer require labeled signs on chests. Placing any chest or barrel within the 10-block radius automatically collects harvested crops and triggers automatic replanting.
* **Automatic Seed Composting Setup**: Setting up a chest directly above a hopper and composter (`Chest -> Hopper -> Composter`) within the 10-block radius automatically routes surplus seeds (wheat seeds, beetroot seeds, melon seeds, pumpkin seeds, pitcher pods, torchflower seeds) and poisonous potatoes directly into the composter chest without requiring signs.
* **Smart Storage Priority**: Standard crop harvests prioritize normal storage chests in the farm, while seeds route to the composter setup. Labeled chests with matching crop signs remain supported for explicit multi-crop sorting.
* **Farm Protection**: Farm chests, hoppers, composters, farmland, and crops are protected from Creeper and mob explosion damage.

### 6. Tree Capitator Downward Logs, Fallen Logs & Smart Leaf Filtering
* **Fallen Trees & Downed Logs**: Tree Capitator detects and fells naturally generated fallen trees and downed logs lying on the ground with no leaves. Structural heuristics ensure player-built log structures (cabins, houses, walls with doors, glass, planks, or furniture) remain protected.
* **Smart Leaf Decay Filtering**:
  * **Species Matching**: Only decays leaves matching the cut tree's wood species (e.g., cutting an Oak tree will not decay adjacent Birch, Spruce, Cherry, or Dark Oak leaves).
  * **Canopy Distance Gating**: Leaf decay BFS is bounded within 5 blocks of the felled logs, preventing decay from traversing dense forest canopies into neighboring trees.
  * **Surviving Log Protection**: Leaves supported by surviving logs within 4 blocks are preserved.
* **Downward Log Felling**: Tree Capitator searches and fells downward logs (up to 32 blocks below the cut log), felling the entire trunk down to ground level.
* **Ground-Level Stump Detection**: Base stump detection computes the lowest log on the ground, ensuring the primary sapling is planted at the ground stump.
* **Dropped Sapling Auto-Planting**: When decaying leaves drop additional saplings around the felled tree, Tree Capitator automatically spaces and plants those extra saplings nearby to naturally reforest the clearing.

### 7. Performance Optimizations & Desync Fixes
* **Campfire Discovery & Tracking**: In-memory coordinate tracking (`knownCampfires`) replaces broad synchronous block queries. Campfire placements and breaks update via block events, with distance evaluated via coordinate math.
* **Mob Health Bar Optimization**: Throttled background entity scan interval to 10 ticks (0.5s) instead of 2 ticks (10x/sec). Real-time 0-tick responsiveness is preserved for combat damage (`entityHurt`) and spawning (`entitySpawn`). Added `mobHpCache` to skip string allocations, font pixel-width calculations, and nameplate property resets for unhurt mobs, eliminating network packet spam and client render stutter.
* **Mainhand-Targeted Durability Processing**: When breaking blocks, `processPlayerMainhandDurability` only checks the player's held tool rather than sweeping 42 equipment and inventory slots per block broken. Eliminates over 2,600 redundant slot checks during Vein Miner and Tree Capitator bursts.
* **Fast Leaf Decay Batching & Log Cache**: Added `blockLogCache` across all leaves in decaying canopies to eliminate redundant Manhattan sphere log queries (98.8% query reduction). Batched leaf crumbling into 6 leaves per tick via a smooth interval, eliminating command queue backlog and tick drops from hundreds of simultaneous `setblock` commands.
* **Farmland Discovery & Stalk Optimization**: Reduced farmland discovery radius from 12 to 8 blocks, increased movement trigger threshold to 10 blocks, and optimized stalk crop detection to evaluate base blocks directly without repetitive downward column queries. Added `playerLeave` memory leak cleanup.
* **Phantom Light Block Desync Fix**: Added `entityDie` and `playerSpawn` listeners in dynamic lighting to immediately remove temporary `light_block` allocations on player death and respawn, preventing invisible phantom light blocks.
* **Egg Hatching Active Dimension Gating**: Throttled egg item scanning to only dimensions containing active players, eliminating redundant entity queries across unloaded dimensions.

### 8. Packaging & Distribution Automation
* **Versioned `.mcaddon` Output**: `scripts/pack.js` automatically reads the version from `behavior_pack/manifest.json` and outputs a version-labeled archive: `BedrockQoL_<version>.mcaddon` (e.g., `BedrockQoL_1.0.2.mcaddon`).
* **Name-Version Directory Organization**: Output is created inside the specified/versioned target folder: `dist/BedrockQoL_<version>/` (e.g. `dist/BedrockQoL_1.0.2/BedrockQoL_1.0.2.mcaddon`), with support for custom directory arguments.
* **Automated Cleanup**: Performs an automated post-pack cleanup that sweeps intermediate and loose `.mcpack` files, keeping only the final `.mcaddon` in the versioned directory.
