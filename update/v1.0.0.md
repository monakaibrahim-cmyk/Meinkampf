# Bedrock QoL Suite — v1.0.0 Patch Notes

Initial public release of the Bedrock QoL Suite for Minecraft Bedrock Edition (v1.21.0+).

---

## What's in v1.0.0

### Dynamic Lighting & Offhand Torches
* **Held Dynamic Light**: Over 50 vanilla light sources emit real-time dynamic light (levels 1–15) when held in the main hand or offhand.
* **Torso & Crawling Fallback**: Light blocks adjust to player stance—checking torso height (`y + 1`) or falling back to feet level (`y`) when crawling or under low ceilings.
* **Safe Water Handling**: Light blocks will not replace or disturb water blocks, leaving ocean and water physics intact.
* **Offhand Torches & Lanterns**: Torches and lanterns can now be dual-wielded in the offhand slot:
  * Torches: `torch`, `soul_torch`, `redstone_torch`, `underwater_torch`, colored torches (`blue`, `red`, `green`, `purple`).
  * Lanterns: `lantern`, `soul_lantern`, `sea_lantern`, `jack_o_lantern`.
  * **3 Ways to Equip**:
    1. Right-click into empty air with torch/lantern in main hand.
    2. Sneak and punch/swing into empty air.
    3. Click the `Torch / Lantern Offhand` button inside the Backpack Sorter menu.

### Farming & Agriculture
* **Balanced Crop Growth**:
  * Standard crops (Wheat, Carrots, Potatoes, Beetroot, Nether Wart, Cocoa, Melon/Pumpkin Stems, Sugar Cane, Cactus, Bamboo, Kelp, Torchflowers, Pitcher Plants) follow the vanilla 1-day cycle (24,000 ticks) boosted by a **1.25x rate multiplier** (~19,200 ticks = 16 minutes to full maturity).
  * **Sweet Berry Bush & Glow Berries Exception**: Both berry types remain on the **fast cycle (100 ticks = 5 seconds)** for rapid foraging.
  * Growth loop runs every 20 ticks (1.0s) to eliminate background tick lag.
* **Cave Vines (Glow Berries)**: Full downward vine growth, berry sprouting, right-click berry picking, and chest auto-harvesting.
* **Right-Click Harvest**: Right-clicking mature crops harvests drops and automatically replants in a single action.
* **Farmland Protection**: Farmland stays at maximum hydration (moisture level 7) without water sources, cannot be trampled by jumping players or mobs, and resists creeper explosions.
* **Automated Chest Harvesting**: Chests labeled with a matching sign (e.g. `[Wheat]`, `[Carrots]`) automatically gather mature crops within a 10-block radius.
* **Compost Seed Routing**: Placing a chest labeled `seeds` above a hopper and composter routes surplus seeds directly into bonemeal production.

### Inventory & Container Sorting
* **In-Game Sorting Menu (`ActionFormData`)**:
  * **Category / Type**: Groups items by tools, armor, weapons, ores, building blocks, food, and miscellaneous.
  * **Alphabetical (A–Z)**: Sorts alphabetically by display name.
  * **Quantity**: Sorts by stack count from largest to smallest.
  * **Compact & Merge**: Consolidates matching partial stacks.
  * **Hotbar Lock**: Toggle to keep hotbar slots (0–8) untouched while sorting.
* **Multiple Sorting Triggers**:
  * Tap/punch a chest to open its container sorter menu.
  * Close any container to auto-sort on close.
  * Right-click the `⚡ Quick Sorter` item anywhere in air.

### Combat, Mobs & Survival
* **Mob Health Bar**:
  * Vanilla pixel font display rendered above the crosshair showing targeted mob name, current/max health, and an XP-gauge style health bar.
  * Detects targets up to 15 blocks away for safe archery assessment.
* **Morning Creeper Despawn**: Creepers on the surface exposed to morning sunlight fizzle out and despawn with smoke particles and poof sound.
* **32-Block XP Magnet**: Attracts loose XP orbs directly to the player's torso from up to 32 blocks away.
* **Natural Egg Hatching**: Laid chicken eggs on the ground incubate for 30 seconds and hatch into baby chicks (with an 8% twin chance and nearby density caps).
* **Auto Totem**: Offhand slot automatically refills with a Totem of Undying from inventory when popped. Totems can stack up to 64.
* **Auto Shield**: Automatically raises shield when sneaking or upon taking combat damage.
* **Campfire Regen**: Standing within 5 blocks of a lit campfire grants passive health regeneration.
* **Auto Fishing**: Automatically hooks and recasts when a fish bites the bobber.
* **Immortal Pets**: Tamed dogs and cats take no fatal damage and auto-regenerate.
* **Durability Freezing**: Tools, weapons, armor, shields, and elytra take 0 durability wear and stay fully repaired.
* **Vein Miner & Tree Capitator**:
  * Sneak-mine any ore to fell the entire connected vein.
  * Sneak-chop a tree log to fell the entire trunk; leaves decay rapidly.
